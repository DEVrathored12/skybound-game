ABOUT THE GAME

What is Skybound?

Skybound is a fast-paced HTML5 flying arcade game. Tap or click to keep your character airborne, dodge the green pipes and rack up points. Simple to learn — impossible to put down.

🎯

Simple Controls

Tap, click or press Space to fly. One button is all you need.

🏆

Beat Your Best

Your high score is saved automatically. Keep pushing your limits.

🎵

Full Audio

Immersive background music and sound effects for every moment.

📱

Play Anywhere

Works perfectly on mobile, tablet and desktop browsers.

HOW TO PLAY

It's Easy to Start

1

Press Play

Hit the PLAY button to start your flight into the neon night sky.

2

Tap to Fly

Tap the screen, click the mouse, or press Space to flap and gain height.

3

Dodge the Pipes

Fly through the gaps between green pipes. Each one you pass scores a point.

4

Reach Score 20

Hit 20 points to complete the mission and win the game!
